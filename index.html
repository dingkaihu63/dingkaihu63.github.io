关于 About

我是同济大学本科二年级的胡定锴，在李国欣老师指导下做外骨骼机器人。 我目前的工作，是让视觉语言模型能在边缘设备上实时运行，并记住它所协助的人。

I am Dingkai Hu, a second-year undergraduate at Tongji University, working with Prof. Guoxin Li on exoskeleton robots. My current work is about making vision-language models run in real time on edge hardware and letting them remember the person they assist.

我对通用具身智能模型、世界模型、空间感知与记忆、边缘智能有强烈的兴趣， 也希望把这些想法放到更多平台上试一试——机械臂、移动机器人、穿戴设备。 欢迎 RA 与远程科研实习的机会。

I am strongly interested in generalist embodied models, world models, spatial perception and memory, and edge intelligence, and I would like to try these ideas on more platforms — robot arms, mobile robots, wearables. I am open to research assistantships and remote research internships.

论文 Papers

  • [1]

    A Sparse Lightweight Intent-Aware VLM for Rapid Locomotion Adaptation in Exoskeleton-Assisted Walking

    在审Under review ICRA 2027 · 第一作者 · 2026.09 投稿 First author · submitted Sep 2026

    以 Qwen3.5-2B 为基座，经结构化剪枝与词表压缩构建轻量 VLM（locoVLM）；将 RGB 与 IMU 数据流映射为 29 维决策向量（风险、地形等级、步态调整、逐维置信度），再由单 IMU 估计的穿戴者意图修正。部署于 Jetson Nano，推理延迟 78 ms，执行错位率由约 60% 降至 13.6%。

    locoVLM is built from Qwen3.5-2B by structural pruning and vocabulary reduction. RGB and IMU streams are mapped to a 29-dimensional decision vector (risk, terrain grade, gait adjustment, per-dimension confidence), refined by wearer intent estimated from a single IMU. Deployed on a Jetson Nano at 78 ms inference latency, reducing execution misalignment from approximately 60% to 13.6%.

    ExoMem: From Memory to Matrix Parameters

    撰写中In preparation · 第一作者 · 2026.08 – 至今 First author · Aug 2026 – present

    面向外骨骼机器人的双层长期运动记忆：步态周期压缩为场景 / 步态 / 意图 / 响应分块向量，习惯偏差固化为原型与习惯范围，仅保留罕见残差例外；群体先验表示为共享矩阵， 个体残差以 rank-4 因子经闭式外积实时写入。数据流压缩约 240×，单用户记忆恒定于 512 B – 1 KB， 读写延迟 0.07 – 0.16 ms。

    A dual-layer long-term motion memory for exoskeleton robots: gait cycles are compressed into scene / gait / intent / response block vectors, habitual deviations consolidate into prototypes with habit ranges, and only rare residual exceptions are retained. Fleet-wide priors are expressed as shared matrices, while individual residuals are written in real time as rank-4 factors via closed-form outer products. The raw stream is compressed by approximately 240×, at a constant 512 B – 1 KB per user with 0.07 – 0.16 ms read/write latency.
