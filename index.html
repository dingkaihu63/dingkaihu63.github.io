关于 About

我是胡定锴，同济大学工科试验班本科二年级学生，现就读于新能源材料与器件专业， 在李国欣老师指导下从事外骨骼机器人与生机电系统研究。 我关心的问题是：如何让通用模型真正进入物理世界——既理解空间、记住环境，也能在算力受限的设备上实时行动。

I am Dingkai Hu, a second-year undergraduate at Tongji University, where I entered through the Engineering Trial Class and now study New Energy Materials and Devices. Under the guidance of Prof. Guoxin Li, I work on exoskeleton robotics and biomechatronic systems. My central question is how to bring general-purpose models into the physical world: understanding space, retaining memory of environments, and acting in real time under tight compute budgets.

目前沿两条主线推进：其一，面向外骨骼辅助行走的轻量多模态决策，把视觉语言模型压缩到边缘端可实时推理的规模，输出可执行的控制向量； 其二，面向长期使用的结构化记忆，以有界、可检索的表征刻画个体差异。相关工作以第一作者投稿 ICRA 2027，另一篇正在撰写。

Two threads currently run in parallel: lightweight multimodal decision-making for exoskeleton-assisted walking, which compresses a vision-language model to a size that runs in real time on the edge and emits executable control vectors; and structured memory for long-term use, which represents individual differences with bounded, retrievable representations. The first has been submitted to ICRA 2027 as a first-author paper; the second is in preparation.

论文 Papers

  • [1]

    A Sparse Lightweight Intent-Aware VLM for Rapid Locomotion Adaptation in Exoskeleton-Assisted Walking

    在审Under review ICRA 2027 · 第一作者 · 2026.09 投稿 First author · submitted Sep 2026

    以 Qwen3.5-2B 为基座，经结构化剪枝与词表压缩构建轻量 VLM（locoVLM）；将 RGB 与 IMU 数据流映射为 29 维决策向量（风险、地形等级、步态调整、逐维置信度），再由单 IMU 估计的穿戴者意图修正。部署于 Jetson Nano，推理延迟 78 ms，执行错位率由约 60% 降至 13.6%。

    Built on Qwen3.5-2B, locoVLM is obtained through structural pruning and vocabulary reduction. RGB and IMU streams are mapped to a 29-dimensional decision vector (risk, terrain grade, gait adjustment, per-dimension confidence), refined by wearer intent estimated from a single IMU. Deployed on a Jetson Nano at 78 ms inference latency, reducing execution misalignment from approximately 60% to 13.6%.

    ExoMem: From Memory to Matrix Parameters

    撰写中In preparation · 第一作者 · 2026.08 – 至今 First author · Aug 2026 – present

    面向外骨骼机器人的双层长期运动记忆：步态周期压缩为场景 / 步态 / 意图 / 响应分块向量，习惯偏差固化为原型与习惯范围，仅保留罕见残差例外；群体先验表示为共享矩阵， 个体残差以 rank-4 因子经闭式外积实时写入。数据流压缩约 240×，单用户记忆恒定于 512 B – 1 KB， 读写延迟 0.07 – 0.16 ms。

    A dual-layer long-term motion memory for exoskeleton robots: gait cycles are compressed into scene / gait / intent / response block vectors, habitual deviations consolidate into prototypes with habit ranges, and only rare residual exceptions are retained. Fleet-wide priors are expressed as shared matrices, while individual residuals are written in real time as rank-4 factors via closed-form outer products. The raw stream is compressed by approximately 240×, at a constant 512 B – 1 KB per user with 0.07 – 0.16 ms read/write latency.
